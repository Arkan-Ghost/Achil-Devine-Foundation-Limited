Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer?

Visit our Volunteer page or contact us directly at partnerships@Achiledevinefoundation.org to learn about current opportunities.

Where do donations go?

92% of donations go directly to programs. Our annual reports provide detailed financial breakdowns.

Do you work internationally?

Currently we work in Uganda, but we partner with international organizations to support refugees from across the region.

How can I partner with you?

We're always looking for partners. Visit our Partner page or email us at partnerships@Achiledevinefoundation.org.

Ready to Make a Difference?

Whether you want to donate, volunteer, or partner with us, we'd love to hear from you.
