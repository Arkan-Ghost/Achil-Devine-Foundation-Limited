Hope in Action

Behind every statistic is a human story. Our success stories showcase the resilience, determination, and courage of the individuals and communities we serve. These are the moments that remind us why our work matters.

From refugees who have rebuilt their lives to survivors who have found their voice, each story is a testament to the power of opportunity and support.

"Every person has a story. Every story has the power to inspire change."

Featured Story
Rose Muheko's Journey
From Survivor to Entrepreneur
"I never imagined I could own my own business and employ others. ADF gave me hope and the skills to build a future."

Rose Muheko fled conflict from DRC, her home country, with nothing but hope, from survival to a life full of resilient and hope.

Rose Muheko, a survivor of sexual violence, once lived in silence and pain, struggling with trust and self-worth. Her life changed when she joined a support program at Achile Devine, where counseling and group therapy helped her heal and regain confidence. Through livelihood training, she started a small food business that grew into her own restaurant. Today, she is both an entrepreneur and a mentor, working with the same organization to support other survivors and inspire them to rebuild their lives.
